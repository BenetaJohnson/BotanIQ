# 🌱 BotanIQ – AI-Powered Crop Disease Intelligence Platform

BotanIQ is a full-stack agricultural intelligence platform that combines AI-powered crop disease diagnosis, weather-based risk forecasting, global outbreak monitoring, and historical trend analysis into a unified decision-support system for farmers, agronomists, and agricultural researchers.

The platform leverages multimodal AI, real-time weather intelligence, and agricultural datasets from global organizations such as FAO, USDA, and CGIAR to help users identify crop diseases, predict environmental risks, and make informed treatment decisions.

## 🚀 Features

### 🔬 AI Crop Diagnostic Lab

- Upload crop leaf images for instant disease analysis.
- AI-powered disease identification using Google Gemini Vision.
- Confidence scoring and severity assessment.
- Detailed treatment recommendations including:
  - Chemical Controls
  - Biological Controls
  - Cultural Controls

### 🌦️ Smart Farmer Advisory Center

- Location-based agricultural risk analysis.
- Real-time weather integration.
- Early warning alerts for:
  - Fungal Diseases
  - Bacterial Infections
  - Pest Infestations
- Crop-specific recommendations.

### 🗺️ Global Outbreak Intelligence Dashboard

- Interactive disease outbreak visualization.
- Region-wise disease monitoring.
- Global agricultural health scoring.
- Real-time outbreak exploration and filtering.

### 📈 Historical Trend Analytics

- Multi-year crop loss analysis.
- Seasonal disease progression tracking.
- Climate-risk trend visualization.
- Agricultural forecasting support.

### 📚 Diagnostic Archive & Reporting

- Centralized disease scan history.
- Search and filter previous analyses.
- Detailed report viewing.
- CSV export functionality for research and record keeping.

By combining computer vision, weather intelligence, and agricultural research data, the platform empowers farmers and researchers to make faster, more informed decisions while reducing crop losses and improving food security.
